Shop-floor quality inspection

Catching defects
before they leave the line

MQI records checked, accepted, rejected, and rework counts for every caliper and master cylinder that passes inspection. Each submission validates instantly against the quality backend, and a running session log stays on this device for shift-end export — a single lightweight page, no login and no build step.

How it works

  1. 1
    Select the part — choose from the standardized list of calipers, master cylinders, and brackets currently in production.
  2. 2
    Enter quantities — record checked, OK, rejected, and optional rework counts for the batch.
  3. 3
    Review the snapshot — reject % and rework % calculate live against target thresholds before you submit.
  4. 4
    Submit for validation — the record posts to the n8n inspection webhook, which checks it and returns a pass or fail status.
  5. 5
    Logged for the shift — every submission is saved to the on-device session log, ready to export as CSV at shift end.
